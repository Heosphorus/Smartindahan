# Data Breach Response Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how smarTindahan responds to suspected or actual unauthorized access or exposure of customer or store data.

## Response steps

1. detect and report the issue
2. contain affected systems or credentials
3. assess scope and impact
4. notify affected parties when appropriate
5. document the incident and remediation steps

## Escalation

Security-relevant events should be escalated to authorized administrators. Follow-up actions may include disabling suspicious accounts, rotating secrets, and reviewing audit logs.
