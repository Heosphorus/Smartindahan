# Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy is a versioned, immutable record in the database, not a Markdown file.

- Read it in the app at `/privacy` (older versions: `/privacy?version=<id>`).
- Initial text: [`backend/src/legal/initialPolicies.ts`](../../backend/src/legal/initialPolicies.ts).
- Publishing a new version: see [../privacy-and-consent.md](../privacy-and-consent.md#publishing-a-new-policy-version).

It is a **draft** that needs the owner's details and legal review before launch.
