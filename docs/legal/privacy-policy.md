# Privacy Policy

## Overview

SariSmart helps small businesses manage store operations, sales, inventory, customers, and suppliers. To provide the service, we process the minimum personal data needed to create and secure a user account and operate the business tools associated with the store.

## Information collected

- first name and last name
- email address
- mobile number
- store name and store address
- store operational data such as product records, supplier records, customer balances, expenses, and sales activity

## Why we process data

We use this data to:

- create and secure user accounts
- verify email addresses and reset access
- provide inventory, sales, and customer management features
- maintain operational records required for business continuity
- protect the platform through audit logs and security safeguards

## Data storage and security

Data is stored in a managed MongoDB environment and protected with server-side security controls, hashed passwords, verification workflows, rate limiting, and secure request handling.

## Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared with approved service providers who support hosting, email delivery, infrastructure monitoring, and security operations. Operational data is retained only for as long as needed to provide the service and fulfill legal or business obligations.

## User rights

Users may ask to review, correct, or delete personal data relevant to their account, subject to applicable law and operational requirements.
