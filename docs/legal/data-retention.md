# Data Retention Policy

## Retention principles

smarTindahan keeps operational and account data only for the time needed to provide the service, maintain accurate records, support business activity, and comply with legal obligations.

## Examples

- account records retained while active and for a limited period afterwards
- sales and inventory records retained for operational and reporting use
- customer and supplier data retained while required for business purposes

## Deletion

When an account is closed or data is no longer needed, records may be safely deleted or archived according to approved retention rules and applicable law.
